# Privacy

Christian's Popup As Tab doesn't collect, store or send any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers: everything it does happens in your own browser.

When a page opens a popup window, it moves that popup into a tab. It never reads what is on the pages.

Nothing it sees leaves your computer, and none of it is shared with anyone.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/christianjbrown/chrome-extension-popup-as-tab/issues.
